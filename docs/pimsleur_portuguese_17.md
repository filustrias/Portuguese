# Pimsleur — Lesson 17

## Dialogue

A man and his friend invite a woman to dinner.

| Who | Portuguese | IPA | English |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Man | Bom dia, minha senhora. O meu amigo e eu queremos ir jantar com a senhora. | [bõ ˈdiɐ &#124; ˈmiɲɐ sɨˈɲɔɾɐ ‖ u ˈmew ɐˈmiɡu i ew kɨˈɾemuz iɾ ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ kõ ɐ sɨˈɲɔɾɐ] | Good morning, madam. My friend and I would like to have dinner with you. |
| Woman | Ah, sim. Onde? | [a &#124; sĩ ‖ ˈõdɨ] | Oh, yes. Where? |
| Man | Ao restaurante Colombo. Há lá vinho verde muito bom. | [aw ʁɨʃtawˈɾɐ̃tɨ kuˈlõbu ‖ a la ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ ˈmũj̃tu bõ] | At the Colombo restaurant. They have very good vinho verde there. |
| Woman | Ah, mas não gosto de vinho verde. | [a &#124; mɐʒ nɐ̃w̃ ˈɡɔʃtu dɨ ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ] | Oh, but I don't like vinho verde. |
| Man | Há lá muitas coisas boas e nós também queremos conversar com a senhora. | [a la ˈmũj̃tɐʃ ˈkojzɐʒ ˈboɐz i nɔʃ tɐ̃ˈbɐ̃j̃ kɨˈɾemuʃ kõvɨɾˈsaɾ kõ ɐ sɨˈɲɔɾɐ] | There are lots of good things there, and we also want to talk with you. |
| Woman | Está bem. | [ɨʃˈta bɐ̃j̃] | All right. |

## Concepts

**Vinho verde.** *Vinho verde* (<Lat>[ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ]</Lat> - "green wine") comes only from the Minho, in the northwest of Portugal. It is young, fresh, slightly acidic and lightly sparkling. "Green" means young, not the colour: there are white, red and rosé *verdes*.

**Dinner.** *Jantar* (<Lat>[ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ]</Lat> - to have dinner; also "dinner"): *ir jantar* (<Lat>[iɾ ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ]</Lat> - to go for dinner).

**Over there.** *Lá* (<Lat>[la]</Lat> - there, at that place) points back to a place already mentioned: *Há lá vinho verde muito bom.* (<Lat>[a la ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ ˈmũj̃tu bõ]</Lat> - They have very good vinho verde there.), *Há lá muitas coisas boas.* (<Lat>[a la ˈmũj̃tɐʃ ˈkojzɐʒ ˈboɐʃ]</Lat> - There are lots of good things there.)

**We, present tense.** The *nós* form ends in *-mos*:

| Portuguese | IPA | English |
|:--|:--|:--|
| falamos inglês | [fɐˈlɐmuz ĩˈɡleʃ] | we speak English |
| comemos juntos | [kuˈmemuʒ ˈʒũtuʃ] | we eat together |
| bebemos vinho verde | [bɨˈbemuʒ ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ] | we drink vinho verde |
| somos americanos | [ˈsomuz ɐmɨɾiˈkɐnuʃ] | we're American |
| somos de Portugal | [ˈsomuʒ dɨ puɾtuˈɡaɫ] | we're from Portugal |
| podemos pagar | [puˈdemuʃ pɐˈɡaɾ] | we can pay |

**Our, your, my, in the plural.** *Os nossos amigos* (<Lat>[uʒ ˈnɔsuz ɐˈmiɡuʃ]</Lat> - our friends), *os nossos euros* (<Lat>[uʒ ˈnɔsuz ˈewɾuʃ]</Lat> - our euros), *os seus amigos* (<Lat>[uʃ ˈsewz ɐˈmiɡuʃ]</Lat> - your friends), *os meus amigos* (<Lat>[uʒ ˈmewz ɐˈmiɡuʃ]</Lat> - my friends). *Falamos inglês com os nossos amigos.* (<Lat>[fɐˈlɐmuz ĩˈɡleʃ kõ uʒ ˈnɔsuz ɐˈmiɡuʃ]</Lat> - We speak English with our friends.)

**Maybe.** *Talvez* (<Lat>[taɫˈveʃ]</Lat> - maybe, perhaps): *Talvez trinta minutos.* (<Lat>[taɫˈveʃ ˈtɾĩtɐ miˈnutuʃ]</Lat> - Maybe thirty minutes.), *Talvez sessenta ou setenta.* (<Lat>[taɫˈveʃ sɨˈsẽtɐ o sɨˈtẽtɐ]</Lat> - Maybe sixty or seventy.)

**Numbers.** *oitenta* (<Lat>[ojˈtẽtɐ]</Lat> - eighty), *oitenta e oito* (<Lat>[ojˈtẽtɐ i ˈojtu]</Lat> - eighty-eight), *noventa* (<Lat>[nuˈvẽtɐ]</Lat> - ninety), *noventa e nove* (<Lat>[nuˈvẽtɐ i ˈnɔvɨ]</Lat> - ninety-nine).

## Exercises

1. Say that you and your friend want to have dinner with her (formal).
2. Say that they have very good vinho verde there.
3. Say that you don't like vinho verde.
4. Say that we're American.
5. Answer *maybe thirty minutes*.

## Informal version

| Who | Portuguese | IPA | English |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Man | Olhe, boa tarde! Eu e este meu amigo estávamos a pensar se não queria vir jantar connosco hoje. | [ˈɔʎɨ &#124; ˈboɐ ˈtaɾdɨ ‖ ew i ˈeʃtɨ ˈmew ɐˈmiɡu ɨʃˈtavɐmuz ɐ pẽˈsaɾ sɨ nɐ̃w̃ kɨˈɾiɐ viɾ ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ kõˈnoʃku ˈoʒɨ] | Good afternoon! My friend here and I were wondering if you'd like to come to dinner with us tonight. |
| Woman | Ah sim? E estavam a pensar ir onde? | [a sĩ ‖ i ɨʃˈtavɐ̃w̃ ɐ pẽˈsaɾ iɾ ˈõdɨ] | Oh really? And where were you thinking of going? |
| Man | Ao Colombo! Eles têm lá um vinho verde da casa que é um espetáculo. | [aw kuˈlõbu ‖ ˈelɨʃ ˈtɐ̃j̃ɐ̃j̃ la ũ ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ dɐ ˈkazɐ kɨ ɛ ũ ɨʃpɛˈtakulu] | To the Colombo! They have a house vinho verde there that's fantastic. |
| Woman | Pois, mas por acaso não aprecio muito vinho verde... | [pojʃ &#124; mɐʃ puɾ ɐˈkazu nɐ̃w̃ ɐpɾɨˈsiu ˈmũj̃tu ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ] | Right, but as it happens I'm not very fond of vinho verde... |
| Man | Não se preocupe com isso, aquilo tem lá uma ementa com imensa coisa boa e assim sempre pomos a conversa em dia! | [nɐ̃w̃ sɨ pɾiɔˈkupɨ kõ ˈisu &#124; ɐˈkilu tɐ̃j̃ la ˈumɐ iˈmẽtɐ kõ iˈmẽsɐ ˈkojzɐ ˈboɐ i ɐˈsĩ ˈsẽpɾɨ ˈpɔmuz ɐ kõˈvɛɾsɐ ɐ̃j̃ ˈdiɐ] | Don't worry about that, the place has a menu with loads of good things, and that way we can catch up! |
| Woman | Olhe, bem visto. Fica combinado então! | [ˈɔʎɨ &#124; bɐ̃j̃ ˈviʃtu ‖ ˈfikɐ kõbiˈnadu ẽˈtɐ̃w̃] | Good thinking. It's a date, then! |

**Inviting.** *O meu amigo e eu queremos...* copies English word order. A Portuguese invitation puts the other person at the centre: *Olhe, queria convidá-la para vir jantar connosco hoje.* (<Lat>[ˈɔʎɨ &#124; kɨˈɾiɐ kõviˈdalɐ ˈpɐɾɐ viɾ ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ kõˈnoʃku ˈoʒɨ]</Lat> - I'd like to invite you to dinner with us tonight.), *Quer vir jantar connosco?* (<Lat>[kɛɾ viɾ ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ kõˈnoʃku]</Lat> - would you like to come to dinner with us?). Between friends: *Bora jantar fora hoje?* (<Lat>[ˈbɔɾɐ ʒɐ̃ˈtaɾ ˈfɔɾɐ ˈoʒɨ]</Lat> - fancy eating out tonight?)

**Praising a place.** *Têm lá um vinho verde da casa que se bebe mesmo bem!* (<Lat>[ˈtɐ̃j̃ɐ̃j̃ la ũ ˈviɲu ˈveɾdɨ dɐ ˈkazɐ kɨ sɨ ˈbɛbɨ ˈmeʒmu bɐ̃j̃]</Lat> - they have a house vinho verde that goes down really well!), *O verde de lá é uma maravilha!* (<Lat>[u ˈveɾdɨ dɨ la ɛ ˈumɐ mɐɾɐˈviʎɐ]</Lat> - their verde is wonderful!) Note *têm* (they have), pronounced [ˈtɐ̃j̃ɐ̃j̃], against *tem* [tɐ̃j̃] (he/she has).

**Catching up.** Saying you want to "talk with the lady" sounds formal and functional. At the table you *pôr a conversa em dia* (<Lat>[poɾ ɐ kõˈvɛɾsɐ ɐ̃j̃ ˈdiɐ]</Lat> - to catch up, literally "to bring the conversation up to date").

**Accepting warmly.** A bare *está bem* can sound unenthusiastic. Say *Olhe, por mim parece-me muito bem!* (<Lat>[ˈɔʎɨ &#124; puɾ mĩ pɐˈɾɛsɨmɨ ˈmũj̃tu bɐ̃j̃]</Lat> - that sounds great to me!) or *Fica combinado então!* (<Lat>[ˈfikɐ kõbiˈnadu ẽˈtɐ̃w̃]</Lat> - it's a deal, then!)

**Slang.** *É um espetáculo* (<Lat>[ɛ ũ ɨʃpɛˈtakulu]</Lat> - it's fantastic, literally "it's a show"); *imensa coisa* (<Lat>[iˈmẽsɐ ˈkojzɐ]</Lat> - loads of things); *a ementa* (<Lat>[ɐ iˈmẽtɐ]</Lat> - the menu; Brazil says *cardápio*); *bem visto* (<Lat>[bɐ̃j̃ ˈviʃtu]</Lat> - good point, good thinking).
